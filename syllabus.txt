CUET PG MBA (PAPER CODE: COQP12) - DETAILED SYLLABUS

==================================================
EXAM PATTERN OVERVIEW
==================================================
* Total Questions: 75 MCQs (All compulsory)
* Total Marks: 300 Marks (+4 for correct, -1 for incorrect)
* Duration: 90 Minutes
* Test Structure: 4 Sections
* Language Choice: Candidates must attempt Language Comprehension in 
  either English OR Hindi. Quant, Reasoning, and DI are bilingual.
